\section{Limitations}
The measurement covers four app presets, 27 colour filters and one retouching service; AR filters that re-render the
face were excluded because their capture pipeline leaks the label (a model recognised them with the face greyed out).
Celeb-DF-v2 under filters was used to diagnose the mechanism and is reported as a development source; Celeb-DF-B and
the DFD filters were read once per model. On the retouching service the published detectors were scored on one shared
crop, not each under its own preprocessing as on the video sources, and the fixed threshold is set on the service's
own originals; eight of the sixteen usable detectors have a 5\%-FPR threshold above 0.96 on these stills and at 0.5
they call 1--51\% of the untouched originals fake, which is why the threshold-free pair statistics are reported first. The retouching result
is a skin-smoothing result: the other three operations of the service are not accused above the floor by any arm, and
the scripted smoothing in our views is the same kind of edit at a strength range set to cover two other services
whose measurements, like the test service's, existed when the range was set; we claim robustness across
implementations and image domains of that operation, not to an edit the views never contained. The per-epoch curve of
the both-class arm (27\% at epoch 2, 9--12\% from epoch 5, with checkpoints chosen on FF++ validation AUC by margins of
0.0003--0.002) is a disclosure of the exposure analysis, which was post hoc; the chain of Sec.~\ref{sec:mechanism2}
that followed it was pre-registered.

The main model carries a class-asymmetric smoothing view: genuine training items receive face-local smoothing and
forged items without landmarks receive only a whole-crop bilateral filter, and the model calls 35, 80 and 90\% of
whole-crop-smoothed genuine Celeb-DF-v2 frames forged at the three strengths. The class-symmetric view removes this in
the FF++-only arms at a uniform cost in misses under heavy smoothing; whether it does so in the full-data model, and
whether that model replaces the one reported here, is [PENDING: Addendum 36, arm clipe5y]. Whether the symmetric view
holds under Effort's tuning scheme is [PENDING: Addendum 35]. The genuine-only recipe is reported as an evasion route
and not recommended. The exposure effect of Sec.~\ref{sec:exposure} is reliable in sign and on the miss rate; the gain
of both-class over no exposure under app presets is within seed noise, and our models' margin over the CLIP baselines
under presets is mostly the base recipe. Every beautified view we tried costs 0.01--0.03 of untreated Celeb-DF-v2 video
AUC against the no-exposure arm.

The main model is on par with Effort and Forensics Adapter on Celeb-DF-v2, DFD and DF40 face swap and behind every CLIP
detector on DF40 reenactment, at chance in the Celeb-DF domain; it is not a reference detector for forgeries of every
kind. The DF40 real videos are the FF++ and Celeb-DF-v2 test reals already used in this paper, Celeb-DF has no
identity-disjoint split, our models were trained on a different crop from the released DF40 crops, and the scoring of
the exposure arms on DF40 is a post hoc extension marked as such. The DF40 rows of the symmetric arm, CADDM and LAA-Net
are not yet scored. Separating lightly retouched faces from their originals stays at 73--75\% balanced accuracy on the
unseen service. The evidence head localises part-level forgeries, not whole-face swaps.

LAA-Net's released checkpoint was trained on uncompressed FF++ frames and its published in-domain number could not be
tested here (no FF++ videos; c23 project crops only), so its FF++ c23 figures carry that caveat; on the other sources
it is run on a crop rebuilt inside our face box with a different face detector from the authors' and is one point short
of its published Celeb-DF-v2 number. CADDM re-aligns our crop with its own landmark model, one resampling more than the
authors' pipeline; on Celeb-DF-v2 the two agree. The published numbers of Effort and DFD-HR were not reproduced by us
(Effort's were not reproduced by others either); the gap has the size seen for the other DeepfakeBench-pipeline
checkpoints on our crops, and the crop was not proven to be its cause. DFDC and DFDCP are not evaluated, and our DFD set is 332 of its
videos, so no DFD number of ours is comparable to a published one; only detectors run by us on the same videos are
compared. Inference latency of the detectors is [PENDING: latency measurement on one idle machine].
