\subsection{Skin smoothing: what an FF++-trained detector learns, and what exposure does to it}
\label{sec:mechanism2}
The retouching result of Sec.~\ref{sec:measure} concerns one operation, skin smoothing, and we trace it through a chain
of experiments on FF++-only models, each with its bars written before training (supplement). All arms share the
architecture, data, steps and checkpoint rule of Sec.~\ref{sec:exposure}; they differ only in which class receives
beautified training views and in what those views contain. No image of any retouching service is used in any arm. The
scripted smoothing in the views is a bilateral filter blended inside a feathered face hull, the same kind of edit as the
service's (face-local, edge-preserving: high-frequency energy on flat skin 0.68 at the top of the training range
against 0.60 for the service's level 90, background 1.00 against 0.96), and its strength range was set to cover the
levels measured on two other services whose renders are in RetouchingFFHQ; the test service's measurements existed
when that range was set. What is tested is therefore robustness across implementations and image domains of one
operation at matched strength, not generalisation to an edit the views never contained. Rows are in
Tables~\ref{tab:v4retouch}, \ref{tab:v4tradeoff} and \ref{tab:v4evasion}.

\textbf{(i) The no-exposure arm reproduces the published detectors.} P0, trained on FF++ with no beautified view,
accuses 54.2, 56.1 and 43.5\% (three seeds) of the Smoothing-90 renders at the 5\%-FPR threshold of the service's
originals, scores the render above its own original in 97.0--99.0\% of pairs, and moves the median $P(\text{fake})$ from
0.05 to 0.97 (seed 1). Thirteen of the seventeen published detectors lie at 36.3--68.7\% and fifteen at 89--99\% under
the same two readings, so the false accusation of commercially smoothed faces is what FF++ training gives by default,
in our pipeline as in theirs. P0 is at the 5\% floor on the other nine operation-by-level cells of the service
(whitening 4.5--7.5\%, face slimming 4.5--6.1\%, eye enlarging 4.8--5.9\%) and dose-dependent on smoothing
(12.6--17.0, 29.6--40.8 and 43.5--56.1\% at levels 30, 60 and 90). P0 trains with label-symmetric Gaussian blur,
down-up scaling and JPEG on half of its triplets, so plain blur or re-compression in training does not prevent the
accusation. With beautified views on genuine faces only (P1) the Smoothing-90 rate falls to 3.7, 7.1 and 4.6\%; with
views on both classes (P2) to 11.7, 7.1 and 6.9\% (paired differences against P0 of $-37$ to $-51$ points, every
interval below zero). The rate holds under every threshold rule tried (1\% and 10\% FPR, a fixed 0.5, thresholds set on
Celeb-DF-v2 or DFD genuine frames). P2 still scores the render above its original in 72--84\% of pairs (AUROC
0.63--0.70), so for P2 the accusation is lowered, not removed. Both arms pay for it on untreated Celeb-DF-v2: frame AUC
$-0.012$ to $-0.021$ for P2 and $-0.025$ to $-0.030$ for P1 against P0 on every seed, intervals below zero, and 5--8 points
of miss rate at 5\% FPR. The remedy is tied to the tuning scheme: inside Effort's SVD-residual scheme the same views
leave 30.0 and 38.9\% (P0 under that scheme 56.9 and 63.2\%), and two epochs of fine-tuning the released Effort
checkpoint with the views leave 52.7\% against 55.3\% without them.

\textbf{(ii) Necessary: views without smoothing do nothing.} The first pre-registered arm removes smoothing from every
beautified view of P2 and keeps colour grades, the fourteen colour filters, whitening, eye and jaw edits, and the shared
degradation. Smoothing-90 is then accused at 55.3\% [51.9, 58.6] and 67.1\% (two seeds), against P0 of the same seed
$+1.2$ [$-2.2$, 4.0] and $+11.1$ [7.0, 14.8], and the render is scored above its original in 98.6 and 99.4\% of pairs. The
genuine-only version without smoothing gives 55.9\% ($+1.8$ [$-1.8$, 5.2] against P0). The pre-declared reading for
``at least 30\% on both seeds'' holds: the effect is exposure to smoothing itself, and no other beautified view
substitutes for it. These arms still lose untreated Celeb-DF-v2 video AUC against P0 (0.913 and 0.924 against 0.938 and
0.938), so that cost is not caused by smoothing either.

\textbf{(iii) Sufficient, but not free.} The second arm makes smoothing the only beautified view, on both classes.
Smoothing-90 falls to 2.3\% [1.7, 2.9] and 5.3\% [4.4, 7.0], $-51.9$ [$-56.1$, $-48.3$] and $-50.7$ [$-55.3$, $-46.5$]
against P0, meeting bar (a) on both seeds. Bar (b) is not met: untreated Celeb-DF-v2 video AUC is 0.904 and 0.926 against
0.938 and 0.938, untreated DFD 0.967 and 0.966 against 0.981 and 0.979. Bar (c), Celeb-DF-B AUC under the presets, is
0.865 and 0.870 against 0.860 and 0.883; the second seed is 0.013 lower and the bar of 0.010 is not met. The
pre-declared reading is ``sufficient but not free'': every kind of beautified view we tried, with or without smoothing,
costs 0.01--0.03 of untreated Celeb-DF-v2 video AUC.

\textbf{(iv) The genuine-only recipe is an evasion route.} Smoothing shown to genuine faces only lowers the accusation
to 0.7, 1.0 and 0.9\% (three seeds), scores the render \emph{below} its original in 97\% of pairs (AUROC 0.095, seed 1),
and looks like the best operating point of every arm on Celeb-DF-B (AUC under presets 0.887, 0.896 and 0.893 against P0
0.860, 0.883 and 0.883). A check written before any smoothed forgery was scored asks what such a model does to a
forgery that a forger smooths. We smooth Celeb-DF-v2 test frames with the whole-crop bilateral filter at three
strengths and with two forms no arm has seen, a $5\times5$ median filter and a 50\% blend with a Gaussian blur, at the
threshold that accuses 5\% of untreated genuine frames (Table~\ref{tab:v4evasion}). The genuine-only arm misses every
smoothed forgery: miss rises of $+56$ to $+63$ points on the bilateral forms, $+38$ to $+40$ on the median filter and
$+11$ to $+14$ on the Gaussian blend, with lower interval bounds of $+49.9$ or more on the bilateral forms, the forged score falling in
100\% of frames and false alarms on smoothed genuine frames at 0.0\%. P0 of the same seed rises by $+24.0$, $+14.3$,
$+4.3$, $+12.6$ and $+5.6$ (seed 3). The arm with all beautified views on genuine faces only behaves the same ($+46$ to
$+51$ on the bilateral forms). The shortcut ``smoothed means genuine'' is what removed the accusation, and it carries
over to smoothing forms the views did not contain. We therefore do not recommend the genuine-only recipe, and the
low false-accusation numbers of Table~\ref{tab:v4retouch} for the P1 arms are to be read as this shortcut. This also
explains the direction of the P1 effect in Sec.~\ref{sec:exposure}.

\textbf{(v) The both-class arms carry a shortcut of the opposite sign.} The same test shows a flaw in our own both-class
recipe and in the full-data main model: a whole-crop bilateral filter on a \emph{genuine} frame is called forged by the
smoothing-only both-class arm in 47.8, 82.3 and 88.3\% of frames at the three strengths, by the main model in 34.9, 79.8
and 90.1\%, by P2 in 3.0, 31.8 and 49.1\%, against P0 at 0.8, 16.8 and 39.6\% and the binary control at 9.9, 35.5 and
45.6\%. The cause is in the training code: genuine items receive the face-local scripted smoothing and pre-rendered
smoothing frames, while forged items without landmarks receive only the whole-crop bilateral filter, so the form of the
smoothing predicts the class and the model learned ``whole-crop bilateral means forged''. This is a class-asymmetric
augmentation, and it affects every both-class arm and the main model. The arm of the next paragraph makes the view
class-symmetric; the main model retrained with the symmetric view is pending (Addendum 36, below).

With a class-symmetric smoothing view (face-local or whole-crop drawn the same way wherever landmarks exist, both forms
on both classes, no pre-rendered smoothing frames), the pre-declared bars were: (a) Smoothing-90 at most 15\% at the
5\%-FPR rule and the render scored above its original in between 25 and 75\% of pairs; (b) false alarms on smoothed
genuine Celeb-DF-v2 frames at most 15\% for every form; (c) a miss rise on smoothed forgeries not more than 5 points
above P0's for every form; two seeds. Bars (a) and (b) are met on both seeds: Smoothing-90 5.9 and 6.1\%, the render
above its original in 53.7 and 51.8\% of pairs, false alarms on smoothed genuine frames 2.4, 5.0, 6.1, 4.5 and 3.6\% (seed
1) and 2.2, 4.2, 5.7, 4.5 and 3.0\% (seed 2) across the five forms. Bar (c) is not met as written: the miss rises are
$+14.8$, $+14.5$, $+14.6$, $+7.6$ and $+9.1$ (seed 1) and $+19.0$, $+20.6$, $+17.5$, $+9.0$ and $+12.7$ (seed 2), against
P0's $+27.0$, $+5.1$, $-16.0$, $+19.2$ and $+3.0$ and $+26.7$, $+6.1$, $-11.8$, $+18.0$ and $+8.1$, so the arm exceeds the
reference by more than 5 points at the mid and strong bilateral strengths and under the Gaussian blend. The reference
itself is the reason: P0's miss rate \emph{falls} under strong smoothing because P0 accuses everything smoothed, with
false alarms of 39.6 and 42.9\% on smoothed genuine frames at that strength, and the bar did not anticipate a reference
that passes forgeries by accusing all smoothed faces. We keep the bar as written and report it as not met. Read without
a threshold, with both classes smoothed, the AUC of the symmetric arm is 0.835, 0.796, 0.767, 0.829 and 0.850 (seed 1)
and 0.849, 0.809, 0.796, 0.853 and 0.865 (seed 2) against P0's 0.848, 0.776, 0.747, 0.852 and 0.868 and 0.852, 0.781,
0.736, 0.855 and 0.871: above P0 at the two heavier bilateral strengths, within 0.02 elsewhere. The symmetric arm is the
only arm that neither accuses smoothed genuine faces nor is fooled by smoothed forgeries, for the scripted forms,
the two unseen forms and the commercial renders. Its cost is a uniform miss rise of 8--21 points under heavy smoothing
at the fixed threshold, and on untreated data a Celeb-DF-v2 video AUC of 0.919 and 0.934 against P0's 0.938 and 0.938
and a DFD video AUC of 0.972 and 0.968 against 0.981 and 0.979 (Table~\ref{tab:v4tradeoff}). On Celeb-DF-B its AUC under
the presets is 0.866 and 0.888 against P0's 0.860 and 0.883, its beautification-specific miss rise $+14.2$ and $+10.4$
against $+17.0$ and $+14.2$, and its false-alarm rate on beautified genuine frames 5.5\% (seed 1). Its DF40 rows are
