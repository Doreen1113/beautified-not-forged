What the numbers mean

  • The face is cropped exactly as in training: 478 MediaPipe landmarks, their bounding box plus 35 % on each side.
  • On a commercial retouching service never seen in training, this model family calls 0.3 % of retouched faces fake and separates retouched from untouched photos with 71–73 % balanced accuracy. Smoothing is recognised reliably; single eye enlargement, face slimming or whitening often are not, and some untouched photos are called filter.
  • Operation probabilities are shown only when the label is filter. The model's estimates of how much was changed did not pass our validation and are not shown.
  • Probabilities are raw model outputs, not calibrated confidence.
